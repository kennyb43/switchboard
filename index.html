📖 How to Play

🎯 Goal

Be the first player to reach the finish spot, marked ⚑. Roll dice, move your pawn along the connected paths, and shift tiles to build your route to it. Press R to roll, Space to end your turn.

  • Roll both dice to get your movement number
  • Move exactly the sum of both dice along connected spots
  • Shift (optional) a tile that matches your dice roll
If you can't move (no valid paths), you'll skip straight to the shift phase.

👣 Movement

▼

Move your pawn by counting steps along the paths connecting spots. You must move exactly the sum of both dice — no more, no less.

  • Follow the lines connecting spots on tiles
  • You can cross between tiles wherever a line runs over the join
  • Choose your path at intersections
  • You cannot backtrack on the same turn
  • Hover a flashing spot to see a numbered route to it, then click to move
Players start off the board — you'll see your pawn in the tray beside it. Your first move enters through the start spot, marked ⌂.

🧩 Shifting Tiles

▼

After moving, you can pick up and relocate a tile that matches your dice roll. This is optional but powerful!

3
+
4
= Move tile "3-4" or "4-3"
  • Click a glowing tile to pick it up
  • Choose a spot: click a cyan space. The tile appears there, already turned a way that fits
  • Turn it: click the tile again to try the next way that fits. You can also press T (Shift+T turns it back) or use the mouse wheel over it
  • Place it: click ✓ next to the tile, or press Enter. To try a different spot, click another cyan space
  • Esc lets go of the spot; press it again (or ✕) to put the tile back
  • Players riding on a moved tile travel with it!
Tiles can be placed anywhere adjacent to existing tiles — the board can expand beyond its original boundaries!

💥 Bumping

▼

Land on a spot occupied by another player? You bump them!

  • The bumped player moves to any unoccupied yellow spot on the board
  • You (the bumper) choose which one; the options flash
  • Occupied spots aren't valid destinations
Use bumping strategically to knock opponents away from the finish, or strand them on an unconnected island!

🎲 Understanding Tiles

▼

Each tile is like a domino with two numbers (e.g., "3-4"). The numbers correspond to dice values.

Tile Structure
3-4

Each tile has up to five spots:

  • Top, Left, Center, Right, Bottom
  • The tile number appears in the lower right

Paths connect spots within a tile, and matching edge spots connect between adjacent tiles.

💡 Tips & Strategy

▼
  • Plan your path: Look ahead to see which moves bring you closer to the finish
  • Use tile shifts wisely: Create shortcuts for yourself or block opponents
  • Aim for green spots: Extra turns compound your advantage
  • Avoid red spots: Losing a turn can be devastating
  • Watch the whole board: Opponents might shift tiles to disrupt your plans
  • Bump strategically: Send opponents backward or onto bad spots
